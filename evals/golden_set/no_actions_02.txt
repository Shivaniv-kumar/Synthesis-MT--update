Brainstorming Session — Future Product Direction
Date: 2026-06-08
Attendees: Rachel Kim, Dev Patel, Carmen Ruiz, Mei Lin

Rachel: This is a pure brainstorm. Nothing decided today. Let's just think out loud. What could we build in the next two years that we'd be really proud of?

Dev: I've been thinking about a fully offline-capable mode. Like, what if you could use the app on a plane with no connectivity and it just syncs when you reconnect?

Carmen: Ooh, or what about AI-assisted design suggestions inside the product itself? Like, you upload your brand kit and it suggests layouts?

Mei: That's interesting. I've been daydreaming about predictive analytics — where the system tells you which action items are at risk of slipping before they're overdue.

Rachel: I love all of these. What about integrations? Could we be the connective tissue between all the tools people already use?

Dev: The challenge is breadth. There are hundreds of tools. We'd need a plugin ecosystem.

Carmen: Or a marketplace. Third-party developers building integrations. That could be huge.

Mei: The AI angle feels most differentiated to me. The predictive stuff could really set us apart.

Rachel: Great energy in this session. Let's park all of these and revisit next quarter when we do the 2027 planning cycle. No commitments today — this was just to get ideas on the table.

Dev: Good session. Lots to think about.
